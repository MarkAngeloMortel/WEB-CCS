About the college

Who we are

The College of Computer Studies (CCS) is dedicated to developing competent, ethical, and service-oriented professionals in the field of computing.

Guided by Lasallian values, we strive to provide quality education, relevant skills, and meaningful experiences that prepare our students for the challenges of a digital world.

Vision & Mission

Where we're headed

Vision

De La Salle John Bosco College is a leading Lasallian education institution inspired by the life and teachings of Jesus Christ in providing a nurturing learning environment for the holistic formation of the members of the community and partners.

How we get there

Mission

Animated by the Lasallian core values, De La Salle John Bosco College promotes the advancement of academic, research and technological excellence, a culture of innovation and vocation, and sustainable community engagement.

Institutional Goals

Guided and enlightened by the spirit of God, we affirm our commitment together and by association to the following:

  • We shall raise further the formation of academic, technological and emotional competence of Filipino Christian Lasallians to make it even more responsive to the needs of the country and to the call for societal transformation.

  • We shall provide the sense of Lasallian identity and belongingness by living the values of faith, zeal in service and communion in mission across all sectors of the Lasallian Family and the community.

  • We shall get involved in the issues of political change, peace, justice, integrity of creation and the church's mission towards total human transformation.

Core Values of Lasallian Education

The DLSJBC Lasallian, as an inheritor of De La Salle's legacy and a collaborator in the Lasallian Mission, shares in the history and traditions of the Brothers of the Christian Schools — marked by a strong spirit of faith, zeal in service, and communion in mission.

Faith

With St. La Salle, the DLSJBC Lasallian interprets, judges and evaluates realities in the light of the gospel and unites one's actions and intentions with God — relying on God's providence while discerning His will in prayer and worship for the salvation of all.

Zeal in Service

Like St. La Salle, the DLSJBC Lasallian manifests a passionate commitment to realize God's will by giving of oneself wholeheartedly to the service of others — especially the youth and the disadvantaged — in gratuity and generosity, creativity and fortitude, compassion and dedication.

Communion in Mission

Rooted in communion with God, which leads to communion with others in building communion in the world — becoming "signs of God's kingdom and instruments of salvation" for all who seek the fullness of their human dignity and a more just and fraternal society. (Declaration 42.1)
