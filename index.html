About CCS

A Lasallian foundation for your tech career

The College of Computer Studies (CCS) is dedicated to developing competent, ethical, and service-oriented professionals in the field of computing, guided by Lasallian values.

Vision: A leading Lasallian institution inspired by the life and teachings of Jesus Christ, nurturing the holistic formation of its community and partners.

Mission: Advancing academic, research, and technological excellence, a culture of innovation, and sustainable community engagement.

Read more about CCS →

Faith-based formation

Grounded in Lasallian values and our motto, Duc in Altum — "put out into the deep."

Career-ready curriculum

Programs in networking, systems, software engineering, and computing theory, built for real careers.

Hands-on facilities

Computer laboratories and project work that let you build, test, and ship real solutions.
